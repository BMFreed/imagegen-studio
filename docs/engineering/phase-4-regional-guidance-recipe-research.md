## Contract and invariants

Only enabled regions enter the recipe, in document order. Lock and display
opacity do not change participation. Each region retains mask identity/revision,
authored prompt-block presence, ordered reference descriptors and the existing
four coverage classifications. Positive channels remain separate from the global
positive; no composition or overlap policy is selected.

Inheritance uses the supplied concrete job negative, with active exact refs from
the captured Canvas negative sidecars. It never uses the unexpanded template or
dormant local negative. A local empty negative remains empty. Absent positive and
an existing empty positive remain distinct. Regional/polarity identities are not
merged merely because tokens or content match. Active refs are syntactic subsets;
this adapter performs no installed-file or unbound-token verification.

Active regional dynamic syntax is unsupported in this slice. Any brace, including
an escaped brace, refuses explicitly. Dormant inherited-away local syntax remains
authored data. Concrete job prompts must also contain no remaining braces. The
adapter neither expands regions nor adds jobs, chooses seeds or invokes RNG.

The adapter synchronously detaches exposed snapshot/job metadata before its first
await. Semantic and allocation checks precede projection calls. Coverage is
projected sequentially through the existing lease and returned with independently
owned alpha bytes. The caller must await preparation inside that lease. No leased
file paths appear in the result.

References remain raw ordered authored descriptors, including under Anima. Their
presence in a private recipe grants no compatibility or execution permission.
Current Canvas family matching is metadata binding, not installed-model
qualification. Existing reference/negative family gates remain mandatory for any
future execution consumer. Smart draft/bbox and inherited-negative policy remain
outside this adapter.

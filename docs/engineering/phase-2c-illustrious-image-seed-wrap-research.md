modulo2³². IMG/OUT preparation snapshots numeric seed/requested policy, and
orchestration publishes frozen jobs/targets. Worker0.14 consumes the uint32 seed
without a high-bit mask. Completion owns the durable project+document cursor;

source-informed self-review, not blind review. New DI profiles use the existing
native image driver. Two generic image-oracle constraints are extended to a
single accepted contribution and zero visibility edits, with neighboring tests.

no historical report/hash is rewritten and no baseline descriptor is relabeled.

exercise MAX→0, nonoverflow0→1 and standalone MAX. Resource verification and

require four semantic overflow RED, eight neighboring GREEN and fresh twelve
restored GREEN. The installed worker is not mutated; previous installed TXT
high-bit causality remains historical evidence, not new image mutation proof.

The independent reader uses stdlib/Pillow and explicit SHA-bound mechanical
